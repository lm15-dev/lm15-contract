A provider is *supported* when lm15 has a registered provider string with live receipts for it, *partial* when lm15 covers some of its wires or models, *missing* when it is in scope but lm15 has no entry. A model is reachable (`yes` in models.tsv) when its provider is supported and the lm15 provider registers the surface the model needs. Upstream listing a provider proves only that the upstream library knows it; it is no live evidence for lm15.
