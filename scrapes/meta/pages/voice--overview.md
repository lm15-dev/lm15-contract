# Voice

The audio endpoints transcribe speech to text. Transcribe a complete recording in one request, or stream live audio over a WebSocket and receive transcripts as the audio arrives.

- [Transcribe a recording](/docs/api-reference/voice/transcribe)
- [Transcribe in realtime](/docs/api-reference/voice/realtime)
- [Schemas](/docs/api-reference/voice/schemas)

For modes, speaker labels, endpointing, and worked examples, see the [Speech to text](/docs/speech-to-text) feature page.
